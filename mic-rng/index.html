Microphone RNG

Random bits come from the raw microphone noise only. Clicks, clock ticks and steady tones are rejected before any bit is taken. No hashing and no browser random generator are mixed in.

Press Start, then allow microphone access. Use a quiet room; the noise is what provides randomness.
