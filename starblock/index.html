Starblock

You get a number. Friends type it to join you.
Type the host's number

You pick the first mission. After that the missions come in a random order. Dig blocks, build bridges, fix turrets and shoot the aliens. Every mission has 3 hidden boxes under orange glowing stones, a bonus goal, and up to 3 stars.

PhoneLeft thumb: walk. Right thumb: look around. Big button: use your tool (drag it to aim). Arrow: jump. Bottom bar: change tool. To build a bridge: pick blocks, look down at the edge and tap.
KeyboardClick to aim · WASD walk · Space jump · 1 pickaxe · 2 gun · 3 minigun · 4 blocks · 5 turret · 6 rockets · 7 freeze gun · hold mouse to use · P pause · co-op chat Z X C.
PowersButtons on the left, or Q E R T G: dash, shield, shock wave, slow time, heal. Each one needs time to load again.
Best with the phone sideways.
