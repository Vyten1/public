Starblock

Seven missions above Earth, in a new random order every run: steal a caged artifact, raid a mothership, survive the Moon's far side, run an asteroid field, defend your lander, loot a derelict station and take down the Hive Queen. Mine blocks, bridge gaps, repair turrets and keep the aliens off you.
